About

Independent Creative Director

Creative Direction · Visual Identity · Visual Storytelling

I am an independent Creative Director working across visual identity, storytelling and digital experiences. I turn complex ideas, stories and identities into meaningful visual experiences — from research and interpretation to concept, visual language, systems and experience.

I work with cultural institutions, museums and heritage organisations, international organisations, foundations, publishers, education initiatives and selected brands. My role is to give complex subjects clarity, structure and emotional resonance — through research, concept, art direction, design, illustration and digital thinking.

One creative vision, from concept to execution. Strategic depth with the clarity of a single authorship.

Based in Italy and working internationally, I have collaborated with organisations including the Italian Embassy in Amman, Società Dante Alighieri, UNESCO, Loescher Editore and partners across Italy, Jordan and the wider Middle East.

My independent visual research — spanning archival material, symbolism, collage and illustrated narrative worlds — informs the conceptual depth and visual sensitivity I bring to every project.

I see creative direction as a way of connecting research, ideas and people through visual experiences that are clear, distinctive and built to last.

If you want to know more about who I am — beyond the work — the best place to start is The Cabinet: a space where I collect the art, music, symbols and visual culture that feed my curiosity and shape my thinking.
