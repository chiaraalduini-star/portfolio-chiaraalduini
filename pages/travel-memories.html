Travel Memories

Places remembered. Places imagined.

Every place holds more than one story. Some belong to the present, others linger in photographs, postcards and fragments of lives we will never fully know.

Travel Memories is a collection of contemporary collages created from archival photographs, vintage imagery and illustrated elements. Historical figures inhabit imagined landscapes; distant moments meet familiar places, and the boundaries between past and present quietly dissolve.

Each composition begins with a fascination for the lives hidden inside old images — the gestures, encounters and untold stories that time has left behind.

These are not illustrations of places as they are, but of places as they might be remembered, dreamed or reinvented.

Artworks for Your Space

Each artwork is conceived with attention to its presence within a space and can be produced using carefully selected fine art printing techniques and materials.

Different dimensions, supports and formats may be explored according to the artwork and the requirements of the project. Selected pieces may also allow adjustments to proportions, backgrounds or other visual elements.

Not every composition is suitable for modification. All bespoke adaptations are considered individually and developed under my creative direction, preserving the integrity of the original work.

For residential interiors, hospitality spaces, architectural projects and selected collaborations, please get in touch to discuss possibilities and technical requirements.
