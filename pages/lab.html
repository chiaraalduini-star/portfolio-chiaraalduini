Independent Research Practice

Lab

Every project begins with a question.

Through cultural research, interpretation and creative direction, I explore how design can become a tool for understanding history, identity, music and visual culture.

Each project is both an experiment and a chapter of an ongoing research into how complex knowledge can become meaningful visual experiences.

01 · Collage & Illustration

The Sovereigns

Four iconic female rulers reimagined as digital collages.
02 · Interactive Visual Storytelling

Atlas of Forgotten Queens

An interactive exploration of six historical women rulers, combining research, symbolism, cartography and visual storytelling.
03 · Digital Experience

TimeMap

An interactive way to explore 5,000 years of history through cities, places, timelines, maps and cultural connections.
04 · Digital Experience

Wonder Madrigale

A digital exploration of the madrigal, music and the world of Gesualdo da Venosa.
05 · Illustration & Editorial

The Archangels

Series of handcrafted digital collages — no AI involved.
06 · Personal Research · Illustration

Mare Nostrum

A single digital work about the Mediterranean, memory, and the crisis of humanity.
07 · Visual Experiment

The Data Merchant

A conceptual reimagining of a historical portrait, exploring the visual language of data, commerce and contemporary technology.
08 · Brand Experiment

Human by Design

A self-initiated brand world exploring tea, patisserie, flowers and contemporary visual identity.
